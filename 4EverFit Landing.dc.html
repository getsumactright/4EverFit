The four-pillar system

Pull one out and the whole thing falls over.

Most programs sell you one pillar and let you guess at the other three. Every 4EverFit plan is written across all four, in the same week, by the same coach.

01

Nutrition

Macros set to your body and your calendar — not a template. We build the week you will actually eat, then adjust it against the scale and the mirror.

  • Macro targets
  • Weekly check-ins
  • Eating-out playbook
02

Strength training

Progressive overload with a coach on the other end of the camera. Form corrected in the rep, not in a comment thread three days later.

  • Live form coaching
  • Logged progression
  • Home or full gym
03

Cardio

Conditioning dosed like medicine: enough to move your engine, never enough to eat the strength work. Zones, intervals, and a reason for each one.

  • Zone 2 base
  • Interval blocks
  • Recovery tracking
04

Supplementation

An audit, not an upsell. We keep what the evidence supports, cut what your cabinet is wasting, and time the rest around your training.

  • Cabinet audit
  • Evidence-only stack
  • Dose timing

How a session runs

45 minutes. Camera on. Nothing to figure out.

03

The week, written down
